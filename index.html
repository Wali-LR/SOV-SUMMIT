Our Process

From brief to delivery.

A structured five-stage approach that keeps every programme on schedule, on brief, and beautifully delivered.

  1. 01

    Understand

    We clarify the purpose, participants, destination, timing, expectations, and operational requirements.

  2. 02

    Structure

    We create a clear programme framework and identify the services and providers required.

  3. 03

    Coordinate

    We manage communication between suppliers, venues, hosts, transport providers, production teams, and other partners.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    We coordinate the final arrangements and support the programme before and during execution.

  5. 05

    Review

    Where appropriate, we collect feedback and support post-event follow-up, documentation, and future planning.

Frequently Asked

Answers to common questions.

A short overview of how SOV SUMMIT works, who we work with, and what to expect from a first conversation.

What does SOV SUMMIT do?

SOV SUMMIT is an international coordination partner for events, executive programmes, and delegations. We connect clients with the right providers across planning, conference production, delegation management, security coordination, media coverage, and travel experiences.

Who does SOV SUMMIT typically work with?

We work with governments, corporations, institutions, associations, and private clients — including executive teams, family offices, event organisers, and production companies. Scope and structure are always adjusted to the client.

What kinds of events do you coordinate?

International conferences, executive dinners, receptions, launches, delegation visits, management training programmes, private journeys, and complex multi-provider engagements. Every programme is scoped individually.

Where does SOV SUMMIT operate?

SOV SUMMIT is the operating brand of Sovereign Summit GmbH, based in Zug, Switzerland, and coordinates programmes internationally through a network of trusted providers in each destination.

How is a project scoped and priced?

Each engagement is quoted individually based on the brief, destination, timing, participants, and services required. After an initial conversation we prepare a tailored proposal outlining scope, providers, and delivery.

How do we start a conversation?

Reach out by email at info@sov-summit.com or via WhatsApp at +41 79 876 35 73. Share a short outline of your programme and we will follow up to discuss requirements and next steps.
